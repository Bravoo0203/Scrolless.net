Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you contact us regarding device quotes and pickup services.

When you contact us, we may collect the following information:

We only collect information that you voluntarily provide.

How we use your information

Your information is used only for the following purposes:

Information sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Your information is only used for communication between you and our service.

Data storage

We keep your information only as long as needed to:

  • Complete your transaction or inquiry

After that, it may be deleted upon request or when no longer needed.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access or misuse. However, no method of online transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Your choices

You may request:

  • A copy of your information

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact us through the same method you used to reach out initially.
