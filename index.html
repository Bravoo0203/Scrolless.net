Features

Usage Limit

Set daily or weekly limits for specific apps. Get notified when you're approaching your limit, and block access when you've reached it.

Downtime

Schedule focused time blocks where selected apps are completely blocked. Perfect for work hours, meals, or bedtime.

Unique
Location Blocking

When you enter a geofence zone, you'll need to open the app to confirm you're out of the zone. Perfect for keeping work apps at work or social media away from home.

Private & Secure

All location data and app usage stays on your device. We never see your data.

Battery Efficient

Smart geofencing that uses minimal battery while maintaining accuracy.

Works with Any App

Block any app on your device. Social media, games, browsers, or productivity apps.

Fully Customizable

Create multiple blocking profiles for different days, times, and locations.
